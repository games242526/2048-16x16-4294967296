How to Play

This is 2048 on a giant 16×16 board, but every new tile is a 1 (sometimes a 2), not a 2 or 4. Slide tiles with the same number into each other to merge them into double the value. The goal is the 4,294,967,296 tile (232) — and you can keep going beyond it.

Fair warning: that goal needs billions of merges' worth of tiles, so treat it as an endless marathon. Your Best score is the real target, and Auto Move on Turbo is how you chase the big tiles.

Controls

Swipe anywhere on the page, or use the arrow keys / W A S D. Undo steps back one move, New Game resets the board, and the ⤢ button (or the F key) toggles fullscreen. Huge tiles are shortened, e.g. 4.3B is 4,294,967,296.

Auto Move

Pick a pattern and a speed, then press Start:

  • Corner — pushes tiles up and into one side, swapping sides whenever that stops working.
  • Swing — alternates between two opposite directions (up/down, then left/right).
  • Swirl — cycles through all four directions in rotation.
  • Random — picks a random direction every move.

Normal animates every move, Fast skips the animation, and Turbo plays as many moves per frame as your device can handle. Auto Move stops itself on a win, a loss, or when Time Rush ends.

Time Rush

Choose 60, 300, or 1500 seconds. The clock starts on your first move, pauses while this window is open or the page is hidden, and when it hits zero the run is over — score as much as you can. Each time limit keeps its own Best score.
